About

I am a Postdoctoral Research Scientist in the Language, Reasoning, and Knowledge (LARK) lab at the University of Colorado Anschutz School of Medicine. My current research spans large language model reasoning, uncertainty quantification, clinical NLP, and AI for Healthcare. Previously, I earned my Ph.D. in Computer Science from the Department of Electrical Engineering & Computer Science at the University of Arkansas in May 2026, advised by Dr. Xintao Wu. Specifically, my research mainly focused on causal representation learning, counterfactual generative modeling, and causal reasoning in large-scale generative models. I obtained my Master's degree in Computer Science and Bachelor's degrees in Computer Science, Computer Engineering, and Applied Mathematics at the University of Arkansas, where I graduated Summa Cum Laude.

If you are interested in causality, generative AI, applications in healthcare, or want to talk about potential collaborations, feel free to reach out to me at aneesh [dot] komanduri [at] gmail [dot] com!
